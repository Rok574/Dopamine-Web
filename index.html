Troubleshooting

Manually reloading the web app
While in the web app, hold the Top/Side button until "slide to power off" appears, then hold the Home button until you reach the Home Screen.
"A problem repeatedly occurred..."
In Safari, reload the page. In the Home Screen web app, force-reload it as above or restart your device.
Reboots to Apple logo after "Jailbreaking..."
Leave your device powered on and unlocked for ~30–60 seconds before retrying; it can take multiple attempts.
Failed, retry?
Often happens if you're already jailbroken. Refresh the page, reload the web app, or restart your device.
Site updates not showing
Reload the web app or go to Settings > Safari > Clear History and Website Data.
Removing problematic tweaks
Set NVRAM boot-args to -safe-mode via an SSH RAM disk or irecovery in Recovery mode.
JavaScript Disabled
Go to Settings > Safari > Advanced and toggle JavaScript on, and use Safari instead of a 3rd-party browser.
